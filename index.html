    How to Play

    The Throne Awaits

    Two champions enter the arena. Reduce your opponent's HP to 0 to claim the throne.

    Your Turn

    1. Tap Roll Dice to roll all 5 dice.
    2. Tap dice to keep them, then re-roll the rest. You get up to 3 rolls.
    3. Available abilities light up based on your dice combo. Tap one to unleash it.
    4. Tap End Turn to pass the dice to your foe.

    Combos

    • Pair — two of the same.
    • Two Pair — two pairs.
    • Three of a Kind
    • Straight — 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6.
    • Full House — three + two.
    • Four of a Kind
    • Five of a Kind — the legendary roll.

    Defense & Status

    When struck, defenders auto-roll three guard dice — every 4+ blocks 1 damage. Some abilities are undefendable. Watch for status effects: 🔥 Burn ticks at turn start, ☠ Poison compounds, 🛡 Guard reduces incoming damage, and ⚡ Charge empowers your next strike.
